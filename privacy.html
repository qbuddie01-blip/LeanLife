Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 5, 2026

At LeanLife Wellness Center, we are committed to protecting the privacy and security of your personal health and wellness data. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our web portal and mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

To provide a personalized health coaching and habit tracking experience, we collect the following types of information:

  • Account Details: Your name, email address, password, and preferred coach selection when registering.
  • Health & Wellness Logs: Daily metrics you manually input, including water intake, fasting windows, sleep quality, outdoor time, screen exposure, and personal journal reflections.
  • Consultation Requests: Booking dates, times, consultation modes, and optional wellness notes submitted to your clinical coach.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data strictly to run the LeanLife companion features, including:

  • Providing your clinical coach with your tracking metrics so they can tailor your lifestyle coaching.
  • Generating automated, secure wellness summaries and analytics.
  • Sending onboarding, support, and consultation confirmation emails.

3. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely in private databases using standard TLS encryption during transit and robust security mechanisms at rest. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information or wellness logs to any third-party advertisers or insurance companies.

4. Your Rights & Data Erasure

You have full control over your data. You can delete your daily logs directly inside the app interface. If you wish to delete your entire account and all associated metrics permanently, please contact our support team.
